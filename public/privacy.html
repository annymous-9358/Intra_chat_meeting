Privacy policy

Intra Chat · last updated November 2026

Intra Chat is a chat app for teams and friends: group and one-to-one chats, voice messages, a walkie-talkie, stories and a step challenge. This page explains what data the app uses and why.

Your account and messages

We store your username, display name, optional profile photo, about text and status, and the messages, photos, voice notes and stories you send, so the people you chat with can see them. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Messages are shared only with the members of the chat you send them to; stories only with your friends (or the close friends you choose).

Health data (steps)

  • What we read: only your daily step totals for the last 7 days, from Health Connect (Android), Apple Health (iPhone) or the Google Health API — and only after you tap Connect and allow it.
  • Why: to show your daily goal, streaks and badges, and your step count on the leaderboards of the groups and step duels you take part in.
  • Who sees it: you, and the members of groups or 1:1 chats where a step challenge or duel is shown. We do not sell, share or use health data for advertising, and do not give it to anyone else.
  • What we don’t read: heart rate, sleep, location history, workouts or any other health data.
  • Storage: daily step totals are kept for about 70 days and then deleted.
  • Stopping: tap Turn off / Disconnect in Settings → My steps, and remove Intra Chat’s access in Health Connect, Apple Health or your Google Account settings at any time.

Location

Location is used only in location groups, to check whether you are inside the group’s range. Your exact position is not shown to other members.

Microphone and camera

The microphone is used only while you record a voice message or hold the walkie-talkie button. Photos are sent only when you choose them.

Deleting your data

You can delete your messages and stories in the app. To delete your whole account and its data, contact the person who runs your Intra Chat workspace.
